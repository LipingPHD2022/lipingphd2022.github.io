I am a PhD candidate in Management Science and Engineering at the School of Management, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), and a joint PhD training student at the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics (IORA), National University of Singapore (NUS). My advisors are Professor Yugang Yu at USTC and Professor Chung-Piaw Teo at NUS.

My research focuses on practice-based operations management and agentic AI. I work on approximation algorithms, agentic AI systems, and robotic logistics, with applications in retail operations and revenue management. I collaborate closely with industry partners. These include [FairPrice Group](https://www.fairpricegroup.com.sg), Singapore's largest retailer with a network of more than 600 touchpoints serving over half a million customers daily; [Pickupp](https://hk.pickupp.io/en/), a technology-enabled logistics platform offering same-day and next-day delivery services in Hong Kong; [OH!SOME](https://ohsome.co.id); [Schneider Electric Singapore](https://www.se.com/sg/en/); and [Suzhou Beibao Information Technology Co., Ltd.](https://www.babyonlinedress.com/collections-all). I also serve as a reviewer for *Decision Support Systems* (AJG 3), *Computers & Operations Research* (AJG 3), *Energy Economics* (AJG 3), and other academic journals.
